Why French can matter

IRCC prioritises French-speaking candidates in Express Entry. A qualifying French result can add CRS points: commonly up to 50 when you have NCLC 7 in all four French abilities and CLB 5+ in all four English abilities, or 25 with lower or no English. French-category draws typically look for NCLC 7 in all four abilities. Points depend on your whole profile and the instructions of each round — a language test alone does not guarantee an invitation.

Which tests are accepted

For French, IRCC accepts TEF Canada and TCF Canada. Results must be less than two years old both when you create your profile and when you submit a PR application. Only approved test centres can issue official results.

The TEF score trap

TEF attestations show several scales. For Express Entry, IRCC instructs applicants to use the values in the "Équivalence ancien score" column — speaking out of 450, listening 360, reading 300, writing 450. The /699 column is used for other programs. IRCC warns that entering the wrong column can cause an application to be refused. Always read your attestation carefully and check current guidance.

How to prepare without wasting time

  1. Build a real base first. CEFR A1→B2 with can-do goals: understand, speak, read and write — not just multiple choice.
  2. Use authentic graded input. TV5MONDE Apprendre and RFI offer free A1–B2 listening with transcripts.
  3. Space and retrieve. Revisit new words after 1, 3, 7 and 30 days; recall without looking beats re-reading.
  4. Produce, don't just recognise. Write short messages and speak daily; record yourself and listen back.
  5. Then learn the task format. Practice the exact section timing and instructions with original questions — never memorise copied past papers.

Common myths

  • "Finishing B2 means NCLC 7." CEFR and NCLC are different frameworks. Only an approved test can establish NCLC.
  • "An app score predicts my exam result." Practice accuracy is not an official score or a validated estimate.
  • "French guarantees PR." It can help your profile, but eligibility and invitations depend on many factors.
