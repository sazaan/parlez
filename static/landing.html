How it works

A routine you can actually keep

Three steps, repeated daily. Parlez keeps track of the rest.

  1. 01

    Talk like you mean it

    Practise real dialogue in café, workplace or interview scenarios. Ask anything, in English or French.

  2. 02

    Practise what's weak

    Lessons and drills shaped by your level and the mistakes you keep making — then saved as flashcards.

  3. 03

    Build test familiarity

    Explore timed TEF/TCF-style questions, then use your results to choose what to practise next.
