How it works

A routine you can actually keep

Three steps, repeated daily. Parlez keeps track of the rest.

  1. 01

    Talk like you mean it

    Practise real dialogue in café, workplace or interview scenarios. Ask anything, in English or French.

  2. 02

    Choose a focused course

    Browse original A1–B2 practice paths in a dedicated Courses view. Expand a unit, choose Learn or Practice, and your selected level stays synced with Settings and the sidebar.

  3. 03

    Explore exam-style formats

    Try timed TEF/TCF-style questions to guide revision. Practice results are not official scores or proof of exam readiness.
